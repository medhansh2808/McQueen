## D. Findings (not errors, but logged)

- **10-fps anomaly (Q12, RESOLVED):** at the same bitrate, 10 fps → 677 ms vs 30 fps → 277 ms loop p50, with the link healthy (RTT p50 46 ms) during both. Cause: sparse traffic itself (jitter-buffer clock recovery + path/NAT scheduling with one burst per 100 ms). 20 fps is impossible from a 30 fps camera (rates are 30/N: 30/15/10). **30 fps wins.**
- **Jitter buffer is not the latency driver:** 25 ms vs 50 ms jitter buffer → statistically identical loop latency (281.9 vs 276.8 ms p50). The ~280 ms is wire/RTT, not the buffer.
